The Story of Noora

Noora Famous Perfumes was created with a simple belief — fragrance is more than a scent. It is a memory, an emotion and a signature.

Our story

Beginnings, Written in Scent

Noora began in a small atelier where our founder blended her first rose accord for a family wedding. What started as a gift for one evening became a pursuit that has continued for over a decade — chasing the exact warmth of amber at sunset, the precise sharpness of citrus at dawn.

Today, that same care shapes every bottle we release, from the first note we test to the last seal we press.

Craftsmanship

Composed in Small Batches

Each Noora fragrance is blended by hand in limited runs, layered note by note rather than mixed to formula. Our perfumers rest every batch for weeks before it is approved, checking how it settles on skin over a full day.

It is slower than mass production — and it is the only way we know to keep a scent honest.

Quality

Ingredients We Stand Behind

We work directly with growers of Damask rose and Cambodian oud, and test every raw material before it enters a Noora formula. Nothing synthetic stands in for what can be sourced properly — it simply takes longer, and we've made peace with that.
